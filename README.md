Personal portfolio website for **Reynalda Syalwa (SyaSya)**, Digital Marketing & Growth Specialist.

🌐 **Live URL:** [https://reynaldasyalwa.github.io](https://reynaldasyalwa.github.io)

---

## 🎯 About The Site

A modern, high-conversion portfolio built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript — zero external dependencies, ultra-fast loading, fully responsive across all device breakpoints.

### Core Sections & Features:
- **Hero & Value Proposition:** High-impact positioning for performance marketing, SEO, and retention.
- **Live Metrics Dashboard:** Ticker highlighting ROAS, managed ad spend, CAC reduction, and organic traffic growth.
- **6 Core Pillars:** Paid Media (Meta/Google/TikTok Ads), Organic & SEO, Analytics & CRO, Lifecycle Retention, Creative Strategy/UGC, and B2B Inbound.
- **STAR Methodology Case Studies:** In-depth breakdowns (Situation, Task, Action, Result) with interactive category filters and detailed strategy modals.
- **Interactive Campaign ROI Simulator:** Real-time business unit-economics calculator (Ad spend, target ROAS, AOV, gross profit, and CPA targets).
- **Tools & Certifications:** Google Ads, Meta Blueprint, HubSpot Inbound, Looker Studio, GA4, Semrush.
- **Career Timeline:** Work experience with quantified business outcomes.
- **Theme Switcher:** Dark Mode & Light Mode with persistent local storage.
- **Direct Conversion CTAs:** WhatsApp and Email integration.
